Research interests

Research

Two complementary directions: mathematical structures and how mathematics is learned.

Discrete Geometry

Perfect colorings, uniform and isocoronal tilings, crystallographic symmetry, flat origami, and graph-theoretic problems.

Mathematics Education

Spatial reasoning, teaching anxiety, self-efficacy, problem solving, and mathematics learning.

Graph structures

Research on topological indices, nanotube and nanotorus structures, and mathematical graph models.
