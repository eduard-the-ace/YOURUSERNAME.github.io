Solved Problems

Friendly math puzzles with step-by-step answers. Click to reveal each solution.

These are original sample problems to demonstrate the interactive format. You can replace them with your own exercises, illustrations, and difficulty levels.

Problem 1

The missing number

What number comes next? 2, 4, 6, 8, ___

10. The numbers increase by 2 each time.
Problem 2

Sharing apples

Matmat has 12 apples and shares them equally among 3 friends. How many apples does each friend get?

4 apples. Divide 12 by 3: 12 ÷ 3 = 4.
Problem 3

Shape detective

A triangle has 3 sides. How many sides do 4 separate triangles have altogether?

12 sides. Multiply 4 × 3 = 12.
Problem 4

Pattern puzzle

What comes next? ● ▲ ● ▲ ● ___

▲. The circle and triangle repeat in an alternating pattern.
Problem 5

The toy cars

There are 7 red toy cars and 5 blue toy cars. How many toy cars are there altogether?

12 toy cars. Add 7 + 5 = 12.
Problem 6

A little logic

All cats have tails. Mimi is a cat. Does Mimi have a tail?

Yes, according to the information in the problem: Mimi is a cat, and all cats have tails.
